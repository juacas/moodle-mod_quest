The project behind the activity

Open tools for more active learning.

Questournament grows from research and practice around competitive, cooperative and challenge-based learning in Moodle.

University of Valladolid
EdUValab

Built at the e-Learning Laboratory.

EdUValab, the e-Learning Laboratory at the University of Valladolid, explores new forms of interaction in education, learning analytics and competitive and cooperative methodologies.

Questournament is an experiment that became a tool: a Moodle activity for making questions, answers, evaluation and recognition part of a shared learning experience.

Research lineage

Evidence always.

Read the project as a starting point for your own course design and research.

Challenge-based learning

Questions become invitations to investigate, explain and create — not only prompts to recall.

Social recognition

Rankings and feedback can make effort visible while keeping the focus on learning quality.

Open adaptation

Inspect the code, propose improvements and shape the activity around your community.

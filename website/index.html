Star features · Question bank + scheduling

Turn every question into a playable challenge.

Questournament connects directly with Moodle’s question bank, corrects objective answers automatically and lets teachers reschedule the challenge arena as the course evolves.

Competition with intention

Make progress feel like progress.

A live ranking can create energy, but the real engine is the feedback around each challenge. Questournament combines both.

  • Scoring that considers quality, timing and difficulty.
  • Rubrics that help learners evaluate with confidence.
  • Individual or team participation, inside the course you already have.
  • A Moodle-native activity you can inspect, extend and share.
